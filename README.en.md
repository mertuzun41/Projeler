# Mert Uzun | Engineering Projects

A portfolio of my projects in electronic hardware, power electronics, embedded systems, and UAV avionics.

I am a fourth-year Electronics and Communications Engineering student at Kocaeli University. As Electronics Captain of the Sarsılmaz UAV Team, I work on avionics architecture, power distribution, board design, and system integration. I am the project lead for a TÜBİTAK 2209-A-funded 4-in-1 ESC project.
